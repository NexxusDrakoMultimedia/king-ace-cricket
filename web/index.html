New match

With no opponent your team bats a single innings. Pick one for a full match: a coin toss, then the second side chases the target, with a batting and bowling card for both.

The same teams, overs and seed always play out the same match.

        Cup: seeds 1 and 2 can only meet in the final, and if the number of teams isn't a power of 2 (4, 8, 16 and so on) the top seeds get byes into the second round. Worlds: the seeds are split into pots, one team per group from each pot, so the top seeds land in different groups.

        Rules

        Off: every team is equal and results are pure luck. On: each point of strength is a redraw of a King per innings. A 5 beats a 0 about three games in four.

        0: no playoffs, the top of the table wins. Otherwise that many teams go from the table into a knockout, seeded by where they finished: the best plays the lowest. Any number from 2 up works; when it isn't 2, 4, 8 or 16 the top seeds get byes.

        Teams are split into as few groups as fit (some can have one fewer). Each group plays a round robin, and the top of each goes through to a knockout: group winners are seeded first, then runners-up, and teams from the same group are kept apart in the first knockout round where they can be.
