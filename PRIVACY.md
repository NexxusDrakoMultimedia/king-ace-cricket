# Privacy

This is what happens to your information when you use King and Ace Cricket at `kac.nexxusdrako.au`. I wrote it in plain words on purpose.

## The short version

- Your matches, teams and settings stay in **your own browser**. They are never sent to me or anyone else.
- I'd like to count how the game gets used, so I can make it better. That uses **Google Analytics** and **GameAnalytics**, and **only if you say yes**. Nothing from either loads, runs or is stored until you do.
- I **never send team names, player names, seeds or anything you saved**. Only fixed categories go out, such as "league created, 5 to 8 teams".
- You can change your mind any time with **Cookie settings** in the footer.

## What stays on your device (always)

The game saves your matches, teams, competitions and your cookie choice in your browser's local storage (keys starting `kac:v1:`). That is how the game remembers things. It isn't tracking, and none of it leaves your device. Clear your browser's site data, or use **Clear all saved data** in **Backup**, and it's gone, so export a copy from **Backup** first if you want to keep it. Clearing keeps your cookie choice.

## Fonts

The page's text uses the fonts Orbitron and Exo 2. I host them myself, so loading the page doesn't contact Google or anyone else for fonts. Until you say yes to analytics, the only requests your browser makes are to `kac.nexxusdrako.au` itself.

## What I collect if you say yes

| | Google Analytics 4 | GameAnalytics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | A web analytics service run by Google | A game analytics service run by GameAnalytics |
| Cookies | `_ga` and `_ga_` followed by the measurement ID, for up to 13 months, on `kac.nexxusdrako.au` only | None |
| Other storage | None | An anonymous user ID, session details and a small event queue in your browser's local storage (keys starting `GA::`) |
| Sent | Events and page views, your browser and device type, and your approximate location worked out from your IP address by Google (Google doesn't store the IP address in GA4) | An anonymous ID, your browser, operating system and device type, and the events |
| Sent to | Google's servers | `api.gameanalytics.com` |
| Settings I use | Ad personalisation and Google signals are off. Page views are sent as my own fixed events | Events are queued and sent in batches |

### The events

These are the only events, and each one is a fixed category with no free text:

- the app opened, and which screen you looked at (solo, competitions, ratings or records)
- a solo innings or a one-off match between two teams was started
- a competition was created (league, cup or event), with a team-count bucket (2 to 4, 5 to 8, 9 to 16, 17 to 64, or 65 and over) and whether team strength was on
- a competition was completed
- a broadcast was started
- data was exported, imported or cleared (and which kind, for exports)

## Your choices

- **Reject all** or **Choose** on the banner, or **Cookie settings** in the footer at any time.
- **Withdrawing** turns both services off straight away. It clears the Google Analytics cookies and everything GameAnalytics stored in your browser, and stops further events. Data already sent stays with the providers, so see their policies to ask for deletion.
- **Global Privacy Control**: if your browser sends it, I keep analytics off and don't ask.
- I ask again after a year.

## Who gets the data

Google ([policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)) and GameAnalytics (see the privacy policy on gameanalytics.com) receive the events above. They are separate organisations with their own policies. Both may process data outside Australia. I don't sell or share any of it with anyone else.
