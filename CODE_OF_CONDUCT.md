# Code of Conduct

This project follows the community standards of Nexxus Drako Multimedia.

> Nexxus Drako Multimedia is queer-run and queer-made. Trans, nonbinary, intersex, asexual, aromantic, bi, pan, lesbian, gay and every other queer voice is welcome here, and actively backed — not tolerated, backed. Transphobia, intersexphobia, homophobia, biphobia, acephobia and any other bigotry get you removed, no warnings and no debate. Trans rights are human rights.

## What that looks like here

- Use people's own names and pronouns, always.
- Be kind and direct. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Don't post harassment, slurs, or "both sides" arguments about anyone's right to exist.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic: the game, the rules and the app.

## Enforcement

Maintainers enforce this in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space for the project. Anything that breaks it can be deleted, and the person responsible removed from the project, without warning.

To report something, use GitHub's "Report content" option, or message the maintainer, Pyra Drake, on GitHub. Reports are kept private.
